## Result

- Ordinary sowing and planting now take the exact `crop_id + variety_id + seed_reproduction_id` identity from the selected crop-structure section.
- The agronomist sees one automatic primary seed/planting-material block and enters only the rate and its `kg/ha` or `t/ha` presentation unit.
- Canonical demand is stored in kilograms. The verified potato example is `12 ha x 3.2 t/ha = 38,400 kg`.
- A company-local technical product is created idempotently only where the existing operation/request/warehouse contracts require `product_id`. It is excluded from global and agrochemical catalogs.
- Receipts, batches, material requests, issue allocations, returns, reconciliation and field history preserve the same exact seed identity and source-batch trace.
- Different varieties or reproductions cannot satisfy the request. Multiple batches of one exact identity are supported.
- `crop_mix` keeps the TZ-242 multi-component lifecycle and is not forced into the ordinary single-identity rule.
