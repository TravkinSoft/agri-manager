# TZ-221 - Final Specialist and Warehousekeeper UI

Date: 2026-07-24

Branch: `copilot-v1`

Status: `PASS`

## Result

The Specialist and Warehousekeeper workspaces now share one compact
master-detail visual system. Desktop keeps the selectable work list on the
left and one scrollable full record on the right. Mobile opens the selected
task or request as a full-screen record with the primary action fixed at the
bottom and no horizontal overflow.

The Specialist card now uses the approved hierarchy: small yellow category,
large concrete work title, prominent field and crop, secondary date, status,
and a compact plan/progress/remaining/responsible summary. Empty values and
unneeded blocks are hidden. Soil and spraying plans show only type-specific
details. Spraying presents plots, area, solution rate, material formulas,
liquid-material total, calculated water, concentration, and final ready
solution in agronomic order. Water is not a material.

Shift reporting contains only current-shift area and one common comment.
Stop reason, weather and a separate variance-reason field were removed.
Normal progress cannot exceed the remaining area. Progress and finish both
use a confirmation dialog. Finish shows a signed variance; the common
comment becomes required when the final fact differs from plan. Both fields
reset after a successful progress save.

The Warehousekeeper page now uses New, Ready to issue, Issued and History
tabs instead of permanent columns. Compact request cards stay on the left;
the selected request opens as one complete right-side record. Preparation
rows show material, category, plan, available, prepared, remaining and
deviation. Tare, Start assembly and warehouse cancellation controls are
absent. A new request goes directly to `Готово к выдаче`; after preparation,
the page shows that Specialist confirmation is awaited. Seed and planting
material positions are excluded from Warehousekeeper requests.
